# Creative Motion Director

## Activation conditions

Primary outcome: Turn a message and brand into a coherent motion narrative that can be produced reliably across required formats.

Use this skill when:

- A promotional video, animated hero, product walkthrough, reveal, or social sequence needs direction.
- A concept requires scenes, timing, typography, assets, and production specs.
- Existing creative motion lacks narrative hierarchy or brand coherence.

Do not use this skill when:

- The task is routine button, drawer, or list micro-interaction.
- The request has no promotional, narrative, brand, or communication objective; if inputs are merely vague, perform discovery before committing to production.

## Required workflow

1. Inspect brand/product assets and define one creative thesis.
2. Build a narrative arc with hook, proof, transition, payoff, and CTA.
3. Create scene boards with composition, copy, motion, duration, and asset dependencies.
4. Design kinetic type, transitions, camera language, sound cues, and pacing.
5. Adapt the concept for aspect ratios, autoplay, muted playback, and reduced motion.
6. Specify production implementation, asset pipeline, and review milestones.
7. Render tests, review timing/readability, and iterate against the communication objective.

## Skill-specific rules

### Confirm these inputs

- Audience, message, objective, CTA, brand system, and tone
- Duration, formats, aspect ratios, channels, and playback context
- Product footage, logo, type, imagery, audio, legal copy, and asset rights
- Production tools, performance, localization, and deadline constraints

### Apply these decision rules

- Keep one dominant idea per beat.
- Maintain readable copy duration and brand fidelity.
- Distinguish product truth from illustrative dramatization.
- Design assets for reuse across required formats.

### Resolve these domain decisions

- Build every sequence around one audience, message, proof, desired emotion, and call to action.
- Define scene purpose, composition, copy, duration, transition, asset dependency, and audio cue before production.
- Keep kinetic typography readable at actual playback size, speed, language, and muted viewing conditions.
- Distinguish verified product behavior from illustrative dramatization and label conceptual scenes internally.
- Adapt composition and pacing intentionally for each aspect ratio instead of relying on automatic center crops.

### Avoid

- Effects-first storytelling
- Unreadable kinetic typography
- Inventing product behavior
- Treating every format as a center crop

## Validation steps

- Review storyboard and animatic before full production.
- Test muted, autoplay, reduced-motion, mobile, and cropped contexts.
- Verify asset rights, spelling, product accuracy, and CTA timing.
- Check render quality, file size, looping, and platform delivery specs.

## Expected output

- Creative concept and rationale
- Storyboard, timing map, and scene specifications
- Asset/audio/localization plan
- Production and delivery validation
